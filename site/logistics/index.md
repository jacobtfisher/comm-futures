    <section id="getting-here" class="logistics-section">
        <div class="logistics-section-heading">
            <p class="logistics-kicker">01 / GETTING HERE</p>
            <h2>Start with the simple route</h2>
            <p>The easiest airport connection is the Michigan Flyer-AirRide coach from Detroit Metro Airport (DTW) to East Lansing. Once you are here, the conference, hotel, downtown, and campus are all close together.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="logistics-card-grid logistics-card-grid--two">
            <article class="logistics-card logistics-card--accent">
                <p class="logistics-card-label">Airport shuttle</p>
                <h3>Michigan Flyer</h3>
                <p>Michigan Flyer operates daily round trips between Detroit Metro Airport, Ann Arbor, and East Lansing. The East Lansing stop is at the Marriott under the skywalk on Albert Street, at <strong>333 Albert Avenue</strong>.</p>
                <dl class="logistics-facts">
                    <div>
                        <dt>Service</dt>
                        <dd>14 daily round trips</dd>
                    </div>
                    <div>
                        <dt>Advance booking</dt>
                        <dd>Reservations open 180 days out</dd>
                    </div>
                    <div>
                        <dt>DTW fare</dt>
                        <dd>$35 one way / $60 round trip online</dd>
                    </div>
                </dl>
                <a class="button" href="https://www.michiganflyer.com/schedule-route" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Check schedules</a>
                <a class="button button--outline" href="https://www.michiganflyer.com/fares" target="_blank" rel="noopener">View fares</a>
            </article>

            <article class="logistics-card">
                <p class="logistics-card-label">Once you arrive</p>
                <h3>Campus + local transit</h3>
                <p>The MSU Union is at <strong>49 Abbot Road</strong>. MSU provides campus maps and visitor information, while CATA operates bus service in East Lansing and offers free on-campus rides.</p>
                <div class="logistics-link-stack">
                    <a href="https://maps.msu.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MSU maps and directions <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
                    <a href="https://www.cata.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CATA bus information <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
                    <a href="https://union.msu.edu/about/contact" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MSU Union information <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
                </div>
            </article>
        </div>

        <aside class="logistics-note">
            <strong>Travel note:</strong> Michigan Flyer schedules can shift because of traffic, weather, holidays, and special events. Reserve your trip and check the live schedule before traveling to DTW.
        </aside>
